One topic, one QoS and one retained flag apply to all four. This page cannot tell a badge scan apart from a temperature reading, so whichever setting is right for one of them is wrong for another — and nothing here will warn you. Retained is right for status and leaves stale events on the broker for the next subscriber to mistake for live ones.

One topic, two messages: the device publishes online itself as its first message after connecting, and the broker publishes offline from this will if the session ever breaks without a proper disconnect. Registered in the CONNECT packet, so its timestamp is when this session started, not when it ended. Nothing on the backbone knows this topic means “death” unless it was told separately — and untick Retained above and the pair is worth nothing at all, because whoever subscribes tomorrow is told neither.
