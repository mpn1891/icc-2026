Result callback

POST on complete

There is no topic here, no QoS, no retained flag and no queue — because the instrument has none of those things. A URL, a header and a switch is the entire integration surface, and the switch is a demo control: a real one would be the lab deleting the URL. Changing the secret to something wrong is how you show the receiver rejecting a POST.

One attempt, no outbox. If the receiver is down the result stays on the instrument and the backbone never hears about that sample. That is the honest ending of this pattern and the reason pattern 5 exists.
